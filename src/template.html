1 · Phase becomes millimetres

ΔdLoS = λ4π · Δφunwrapped

One full colour cycle (2π) is λ/2 of motion along the line of sight: 11.92 cm in L-band and 4.68 cm in S-band. Here Δd > 0 means the ground moved toward the satellite. The TERRAPULSE vertex shader applies this to every vertex of the mesh, 60 times a second.

2 · Line of sight & 3D fusion

ê = (sinθ·sinα, sinθ·cosα, cosθ)   x̂ = (AᵀWA)−1AᵀW·d

NISAR looks left, so α = heading + 90°. Ascending and descending line-of-sight phase, plus azimuth offsets (multiple-aperture InSAR), give four equations for (Ue, Un, Uz). A near-polar orbit sees north–south motion poorly, so the inspector reports σn in centimetres while σe and σz stay at millimetres.

3 · Mogi magma source

uz = (1−ν)·ΔV·dπ·R³   ur = (1−ν)·ΔV·rπ·R³   R² = r² + d²

A point pressure source in an elastic half-space (ν = 0.25). The Rainier drill injects up to 9×10⁷ m³ at 6 km depth. That produces concentric fringes and lifts the summit by about 60 cm.

4 · A locked fault

v(x) = Vπ arctanxD   u(x) = sπ arctanDx
M₀ = μ·L·D·s̄   Mw = ⅔(log₁₀M₀ − 9.1)   Δσ = 2π·μ·s̄D

Interseismic loading (V = 34 mm/yr, locking depth D = 15 km) and coseismic slip of a vertical strike-slip fault (screw dislocation). Since 1857 the Carrizo segment has stored about 5.7 m of slip deficit, which equals an Mw ≈ 7.9 earthquake.

5 · Coherence & change detection

γ̂ = |Σ S₁·S₂*|√(Σ|S₁|² · Σ|S₂|²)   σφ = √(1−γ²)γ·√(2L)

CCD simulates three complex SLCs, estimates γ̂ in 5×5 windows with integral images, and flags pixels where Δγ = γ̂pre − γ̂co > 0.35. Naturally incoherent water and forest stay quiet because they were already incoherent before the event. The phase noise added in the shaders follows the Cramér–Rao bound σφ.

6 · Freeman–Durden decomposition

fv = 3⟨|HV|²⟩   Ps = fs(1+|β|²)   Pd = fd(1+|α|²)   Pv = 8fv3

The volume part is removed first. The sign of Re⟨HH·VV*⟩ − fv/3 then selects surface-dominant (α = −1) or double-bounce-dominant (β = 1) scattering. Red = double bounce (flooded trunks, walls), green = volume (canopy), blue = surface (soil, water, ice). It is computed for every pixel in both bands.

7 · Why NISAR carries two radars

S-band (9.37 cm) scatters in leaves and thin branches, so it maps the canopy. L-band (23.85 cm) passes through to trunks and to the ground. Over standing water the trunk and the water form a corner reflector (double bounce), so L-band keeps its phase for weeks while S-band decorrelates within days. In this model, biomass from HV backscatter saturates near 250 t/ha in L-band and near 90 t/ha in S-band.

8 · Water under the canopy

Δhwater = λ · Δφ4π · cosθ

Double-bounce phase in flooded forest measures changes in water level (Alsdorf et al., 2000; Wdowinski et al., 2004). The X-ray lens shows exactly that: inside the lens L-band reaches the water, outside it S-band sees only leaves.

9 · Phase unwrapping

φk = φk−1 + W(ψk − ψk−1),   W(x) ∈ (−π, π]

A quality-guided flood fill (max-heap on coherence) integrates the wrapped phase ψ from a stable reference pixel. It always crosses the most coherent pixel next, so the Itoh condition holds where the data allow. The animation shows the unwrapping front sweeping the scene, and the HUD reports RMS error against the true field.

10 · Hydrology & infrastructure

flooded ⇔ hwater(t) ≥ zspill   β = Δs / L > 1/500

A priority-flood (Barnes et al., 2014) gives every cell the lowest water level that can reach it, so levees really hold the water back. In Mexico City, the 10-year angular distortion β of every city block is checked against Bjerrum's (1963) limit for buildings where cracking is not acceptable.

Data, honestly

Real: every elevation model (USGS 3DEP, SRTM, INEGI via AWS Terrain Tiles; Copernicus GLO-90 for Greenland and the Danube Delta), NISAR orbit and band parameters, and all equations above. Simulated: the deformation, backscatter, coherence and events. They are forward models with literature-scale parameters, built to rehearse what NISAR measures. Bring your own: tools/csar_pack.py turns ASF HyP3 / NISAR GUNW GeoTIFFs into a CSAR-PACK that this app loads with drag and drop. Fringes, coherence and the 3D mesh then come from the real interferogram.
